// research interests

Exploring the population genomics, epigenomics, and transcriptomics of malaria parasites and mosquito vectors endemic to India, with a focus on how genomic diversity and regulatory mechanisms influence disease transmission and resistance. As India bears the highest malaria burden in Asia and aims for elimination by 2030, the goal is to integrate whole-genome sequencing, multi-omics data, and computational biology to study parasite drug resistance, mosquito insecticide resistance, and host–pathogen–vector interactions — contributing towards novel molecular targets, surveillance frameworks, and India's malaria elimination programme.
